SaddleTrace

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

The short version: SaddleTrace collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics. Your scans, photographs and measurements stay on your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud storage if you choose to enable it. Nothing is ever sent to the developer.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the SaddleTrace iPhone app ("SaddleTrace", "the app") handles information. SaddleTrace is developed and published by Michael Prange ("the developer", "we", "us").

Information we collect

None.

SaddleTrace has no user accounts, no registration, no login, and no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services of any kind. The app contains no third-party software development kits that gather information. We do not receive your name, your email address, your location, your device identifiers, or any record of how you use the app.

Because we collect no information, there is nothing for us to sell, share, disclose, or hand over in response to a request.

Information the app creates on your device

SaddleTrace does create data — it simply keeps that data on your device rather than sending it anywhere. When you record a scan, the app stores the following in its own private storage area on your iPhone:

WhatWhy it exists
Photographs taken during a scanThe colour photograph is mapped onto the three-dimensional surface so you can see what you measured.
LiDAR depth and confidence dataThis is the actual measurement — the shape of the animal's back in three dimensions.
Derived models, cross-sections and measurementsThe 3D surface, spine curve, section outlines, charts and PDF report calculated from the scan.
Animal records you type inThe name and details you enter so scans can be filed under the right animal.
App settingsYour preferences, such as inches or centimetres, section spacing and page size.

This data belongs to you. It is stored in the app's sandboxed container, which other apps on your iPhone cannot read.

Camera and LiDAR access

SaddleTrace asks for permission to use the camera because measuring an animal's back requires the camera and the LiDAR scanner. The camera is used only while a capture screen is open, and only to record the scan you deliberately take. SaddleTrace does not access your photo library, and photographs taken in the app are not added to it.

You can withdraw camera permission at any time in Settings ▸ SaddleTrace on your iPhone. Scanning will stop working, but scans you have already taken remain readable.

iCloud

SaddleTrace can store your scans in your own private iCloud Drive container, under your Apple Account. This is optional and controlled by you through your device's iCloud settings.

When it is enabled, Apple synchronises and backs up your scans, and can free up space on your iPhone by keeping copies in iCloud and downloading them again when you open a scan. This is a service between you and Apple. Your data goes to your own private storage — the developer has no access to it, receives no copy of it, and cannot see that it exists. Apple's handling of it is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

If iCloud is not available or not enabled, SaddleTrace simply stores everything locally on your device instead.

Sharing scans and exports

SaddleTrace can export a scan, or your whole library, as a report, a CAD file or a single shareable archive. Sharing only ever happens when you tap a share button and choose a destination yourself, using the standard iOS share sheet.

Once you send a file somewhere — by email or message, to another app, or to a cloud service — it is handled under the privacy policy of whatever service you chose. Please bear that in mind when sending scans to a colleague, a saddle maker or a print shop.

Network use

SaddleTrace has no server component. The app does not send your scans, photographs, measurements or usage information to the developer or to any third party. All processing — building the 3D surface, fitting the spine, extracting cross-sections and generating the report — happens on your iPhone.

The only network activity associated with the app is iCloud synchronisation, described above, which is carried out by iOS itself between your device and your own Apple Account.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual scans and animal records inside the app. Deleting SaddleTrace from your iPhone removes the app's local data with it. If you have used iCloud, copies held in your iCloud Drive are managed through your Apple Account — see Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud on your device.

There is no data held by the developer for you to request, correct or delete, because none is ever collected.

Children

SaddleTrace is a professional measuring tool intended for adults working with horses. It is not directed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR in Europe and the UK, and the CCPA in California, give you rights over personal information a company holds about you — to see it, correct it, delete it, or object to its sale. Those rights are satisfied here in the simplest possible way: the developer holds no personal information about you at all, and none is sold or shared.

Changes to this policy

If SaddleTrace ever changes in a way that affects this policy, the revised policy will be published on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
