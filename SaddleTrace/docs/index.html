SaddleTrace

Measure a horse's back with a single LiDAR shot.

SaddleTrace turns the LiDAR scanner in your iPhone into a precision measuring tool for a horse's back. Stand beside the animal, frame the saddle area, and take one shot. In that instant the app captures a dense three-dimensional surface at true scale and paints it with the photograph, so you can see exactly what you measured.

No sweeping the phone along the horse, no waiting on a server, and no stitching that drifts when the animal shifts its weight.

From a single shot

  • A full-colour 3D model of the back you can rotate and inspect
  • The spine traced automatically by left and right symmetry
  • Cross-sections stepping back from the withers at your chosen spacing
  • Topline and rocker profile along the spine
  • Width and tree angles at every station
  • A print-ready cross-section report at an exact, stated scale

Exports for the bench and the CAD seat

PDF report, DXF outlines with one layer per station, CSV of sections and measurements, STL for CAD and 3D printing, and a colour PLY of the painted surface. Send a single scan or your whole library to a colleague as one file, which they import and view exactly as you see it.

Your data stays yours

No account, no sign-in, no analytics. Scans live on your iPhone and, if you enable it, in your own private iCloud Drive. Nothing is ever sent to the developer.

Requirements

An iPhone with a LiDAR scanner — iPhone 12 Pro or later, Pro and Pro Max models — running a current version of iOS.

What SaddleTrace is. A measuring instrument for people who already know how to fit a saddle. It does not recommend a saddle, and it is not a veterinary or diagnostic tool.
